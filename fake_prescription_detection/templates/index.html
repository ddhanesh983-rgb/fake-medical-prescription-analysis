How The Screening Engine Works

A comprehensive 4-step pipeline designed for high-throughput pharmacy intake screening.

01.
Image Preprocessing

OpenCV applies CLAHE contrast adjustment, bilateral noise suppression, and adaptive thresholding to maximize character legibility.

02.
Tesseract OCR

Transforms visual prescription text into structured character streams with layout segmentation.

03.
Pattern & NLP Rules

Identifies physician registration identifiers, dates, high-risk Schedule II opioids, and missing physical signature stamps.

04.
ML Classification

Scikit-learn TF-IDF classifier assesses corpus similarity to generate a calibrated risk score and downloadable PDF audit report.

Academic Screening Tool Disclaimer

This application is developed as an academic project for a Bachelor of Science in Data Science. It serves exclusively as a triage support tool to highlight anomalous or missing information. The system does not claim or certify definitive guilt or authenticity; final dispensing decisions remain the exclusive responsibility of certified healthcare professionals.
